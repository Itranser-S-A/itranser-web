Privacy Policy

At Itranser — Informática de Transportes y Servicios, S.A.U. we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the responsible use of your personal data.

This English translation is provided for information purposes only. The binding version is the Spanish original.

1. Data controller

The data controller is Itranser, Informática de Transportes y Servicios, S.A.U., with registered address at [company address] and contact email: info@itranser.com.

2. Data we collect

This website does not collect personal data on its own: it has no forms, user accounts or analytics. The only preferences stored (your choice on the cookie notice) live in your browser's local storage and are never sent to our servers (see our Cookies Policy).

If you contact us by email (for example at info@itranser.com) to request information about our services, we will process the data you provide voluntarily (name, email address, phone number, company and message content).

3. Purpose of processing

Data is used exclusively to:

  • Respond to your enquiries and information requests sent by email.
  • Provide and improve our software development services.
  • Meet legal or contractual obligations.

4. Legal basis

The legal basis for processing is your consent, given voluntarily when you send us an enquiry by email, and our legitimate interest in handling the pre-contractual relationship and keeping the website secure and operational. We do not carry out automated decision-making or profiling.

5. Recipients and processors

We do not disclose your data to third parties for their own purposes. The following providers act for Itranser as data processors:

  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.): hosting of this static website.
  • Provider of the corporate email service.

Where a provider is located outside the European Economic Area, international data transfers are covered by the appropriate safeguards required by Chapter V of the GDPR (standard contractual clauses and, where applicable, the EU-EEA-U.S. Data Privacy Framework).

6. Data retention

Email enquiries are kept for as long as necessary to deal with them and, where applicable, for the statutory limitation periods for commercial and tax obligations. Preferences stored in your browser (cookie notice) remain until you clear your browsing data.

7. Your rights

You may exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction of processing and portability by emailing info@itranser.com with "Data Protection" in the subject line.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD, www.aepd.es), particularly if you consider you have not obtained satisfaction in the exercise of your rights.

8. Data security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, loss or destruction.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in our practices or in applicable law. We recommend reviewing it periodically.

10. Contact

For any question or complaint related to privacy, you can write to info@itranser.com.

11. Cookies

This website does not use tracking, analytics or advertising cookies. It only employs technical local storage to remember your choice on the cookie notice. Full details are available in our Cookies Policy.

Applicable law

This policy is governed by the European Union's General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Spain's Organic Law on Personal Data Protection and Digital Rights Guarantees (LOPDGDD).
