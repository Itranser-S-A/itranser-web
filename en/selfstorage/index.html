FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What self-storage owners usually ask before switching.

Does it invoice automatically?

Yes. Monthly billing is issued in batches with a unified receipt covering rent, deposit and insurance, applying the VAT rate that corresponds to each line, with continuous numbering and no duplicates.

Can I draw the floor plan of my facility?

Yes. SelfStorage includes a plan editor to draw your facility with corridors, walls and pillars. Each unit is colored by real-time status: available, reserved, occupied, in arrears or under maintenance.

Can you migrate my data from my old program?

Yes. We have completed a full migration from a legacy Access / Store-IT system: over 2,600 customers and 89,000+ historical documents were imported with the original document numbering kept intact.

Is there a portal where customers can book a unit online?

Yes. The public booking portal shows available units with their size and price in real time. Customers book online 24/7, even with the office closed, and the reservation appears in the back office immediately.

Are contracts signed digitally?

Yes. The customer signs on a tablet at the office and the SEPA direct debit mandate is included. The system then generates the official contract as a PDF, ready to send.

Does it work on mobile and tablet?

Yes. SelfStorage is a responsive web application: it works from any device with a browser — desktop, laptop, tablet or mobile — with no installation required.
